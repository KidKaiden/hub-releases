Hub 0.23.0

New: Simulator Arrows: a new page with every arrow as a live preview, drawn exactly as it looks in a fight with its trail, on a clean black stage. Tap one for a close-up with its stats, abilities, forms and a lore paragraph (made up for fun, edit them whenever). Tap an ability to watch a demo of it: the passive, the ultimate, or each one for the Chosen One (Blue, Red, Purple) and Instinct (Flurry and the legendary counter). Fight this arrow takes you to a fight generator like a fighting game's champion select: pick two arrows and an arena, watch the fight, and you are back at the picking, with a Make a tournament button for playing and betting with friends. Nothing is saved and no win records change. Improved: Simulator Tournament page on a wide PC window: one studio with three columns that uses the whole window. The settings that make the simulation are on the left, the preview in the middle and the download and posting settings on the right, so the flow reads left to right and everything is one scroll away. The Factory, Publish and Sound pages use the wide window too (columns instead of one long strip). On a narrow window or a phone they keep the single column and the two steps. The player controls are redone as one tidy row: play, back, next, replay, the time, the shape, sound and full screen

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
