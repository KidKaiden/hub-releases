Hub 0.9.9

New: Messages: a real chat app. Your open chats with how many are new, and chats that update the moment the other person sends; Chat with one friend or a group, reply to a message, change or delete your own, and see when it's been seen; A note pops up when a message arrives while you're in another app; mute any chat from its menu; Connect your own bank in Finance > Banking: ask the owner, and once they say yes your payments come in by themselves, matched to your bills; For the owner: bank requests show in Hub > Admin, with Allow, Say no and Take away. Improved: Everything shared (lists, business, messages) now reaches other devices straight away while the server is on, not within a minute; To Do > People: chat with someone in one tap; reminders stay there; Business: a new business sets itself up step by step (how it's set up, categories, invoices or products, first money in, bank and reminders). Skip any step: what's left stays as a checklist; Business: each business has its own bar along the top (Overview, Money, Invoices, Clients, Reports, More) in place of the tiles at the bottom; Business: money out stands next to money in. Tap In or Out to see what's in it, and see where the money goes by category right on the page

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
