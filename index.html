Hub 0.13.0

New: Everything is encrypted: your Hub's data, backups and keys on the PC (the key kept by Windows), and what each phone and PC keeps (the key kept by Windows, the phone's secure chip, or the browser); A recovery key, the only way back if the PC dies or Windows is reinstalled. Hub asks you to save it once: Hub > Security; Hub > Security: two-step sign-in with a free authenticator app and backup codes, your devices (sign one out, or every other), what happened lately, and what to do if something goes wrong; Secure connections only: phones use your Hub's https address at home too, and move there by themselves; Update the server from the Hub: Updates > Server on this PC shows what's new and updates in a few minutes, going back by itself if anything fails; Hub > Setup: every set-up you do yourself, step by step, with why, a button to the right screen, and Check it; Optional app lock: a PIN to open Hub on a device, and the fingerprint on Android (Security > App lock). Improved: Before something important (keys, accounts, a roll back, real money) Hub asks for your password again, once every 10 minutes; Alerts when a new device signs in, when someone tries many wrong passwords, and when a key changes; Devices unused for 90 days sign out by themselves; only the Hub's own apps can talk to your server

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
