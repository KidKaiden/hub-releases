Hub 0.30.0

New: Trading: a Demo switch in the corner shows the whole app working on a made-up $1,000 account (trades arrive, Approve and the kill switch work, a pretend Lab run). It only appears when nothing is connected, says it is made up, and never touches your broker, keys or the Hub home page.; Trading: Markets. A screener, the US economy (needs your free FRED key in Setup), world growth and inflation, the release calendar, news, prediction-market odds (look only) and a day-by-day replay that uses the same signal and risk check as the paper bot.. Improved: Every Trading page now fills the screen: wide windows get columns, empty pages show what will appear, and nothing clips while you resize. Buttons and switches are 40 px high on touch screens.. Fixed: Trading: the Lab report window's Close button was covered on a PC and the phone's Back button left the page; the Journal could show one day twice after midnight UTC; the phone's More page now opens Bots on a wide window.

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
