Hub 0.23.4

New: Simulator on a phone turned sideways: the video fills the screen with its buttons beside it (using the empty sides), and the settings come after it as you scroll. Back to page brings the usual bars back. A wide window with the tall video also puts the buttons beside it. Improved: Simulator on a phone, held upright: the video is much bigger and the buttons fit on one row under it, so the whole player is on screen at once, with the settings under it. The time showed as a bare colon; it now reads 0:00 / 0:42. Fixed: Simulator Publish page: it no longer breaks when the app has updated but the server on the PC has not yet (0.23.3's new audience settings did that). It says the server is older and carries on with the old settings until it is updated; Simulator: the arrows gallery shows two to a row on a phone instead of one huge picture each. The Sound page no longer scrolls sideways (a slider's value bubble stuck out past the edge; it affected every slider in the apps)

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
