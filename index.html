Hub 0.9.2

New: Check for updates now installs the update in one go. On Android 12 and later there is no screen to tap; This note: a short list of what changed after each update. Close it with X, or turn it off in Updates. Improved: Updates are manual by default: nothing downloads until you press Check for updates. Automatic is an option; After a phone update, a notification says Hub is updated; tap it to open the new version

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
