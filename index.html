Hub 0.22.0

Improved: Simulator export, rebuilt: it starts from the aspect ratio (PC 16:9 and Vertical 9:16, each on or off), then under each you pick Fast cut, Long or both, then separate clips, the full tournament or both. Fast cut can be fights alone or with the intro and ending. Resolution and frame rate are chosen last, for each ratio. You choose a folder (remembered); each tournament gets its own folder with Fast cut and Long folders, and PC and Vertical inside. Where a device cannot write into a folder you get one zip with the same folders. Files are named simply: 01 Intro, 02 Fight 1 Lumen v Gale, 03 Fight 2 ... Finale, Full tournament; Simulator Publish: choose which social media each group goes to (for example Vertical Fast cut to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube; PC Long to YouTube and Facebook), set a time for each clip, or post them all at the same time; Simulator Factory, rebuilt and joined to posting: it now makes tournaments from start to finish with nobody watching. Pick how many tournaments, what to make (the same aspect ratio, Fast cut and Long, separate clips or full choices as everywhere else) and your folder, then turn on Post automatically and choose the social media for each group and the times. It simulates, draws, saves each tournament in its own folder, sends every clip to your PC's queue with its caption and gives it a time, and the server posts them by itself. There is also a Keep going option that starts another batch some hours later while the app stays open; Simulator title, winner, champion, versus and every other card: a new look to match the arenas and arrows. A neon grid floor rolling toward you, a glowing horizon, drifting stars and sparks, a lit frame with corner brackets, rays behind the winner and the champion, glowing names and a bright seam where the two fighters meet; Simulator fights: arrows no longer circle each other for long. After 3 full circles (usually), 5 or 7 (rarely) they break away to get a run-up. Ascendant's Wave Beam and Godfire's Dragon Fist now disengage first, back away, stand and aim, then fire, like the Chosen One. The beams are brighter: yellow in the base forms, blue and bigger as the forms get stronger (Instinct's is still the biggest)

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
