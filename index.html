Hub 0.24.7

Improved: Simulator, pierce shots rebuilt for every arrow as a sequence with clear beats, still at the picture's own scale. Lock-on: chevrons flow along the line to the target, a diamond reticle closes in on it and ticks, and light gathers into the arrow. The dash: streaks flank the arrow and the air folds back off its tip in a V. The impact: a flash and shake, then a star of spikes that grows and fades, rings in both of the arrow's colours, and a thin bright cut left through the target along the dash. The impact also takes a flavour from each arrow's own character: arcs for electric arrows, a cross flare and sparks for metal, shards for glass and crystal, droplets for water, a spiral shear for air, cracks in the ground for the heavy arrows, a radiant halo for the bell-voiced ones, and splinters for wood
