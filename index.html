Hub 0.24.6

New: Simulator, a new arrow: Sniper. It fights with its tip like any arrow. After its 5th hit it takes four shots, one at a time: it runs to a far place, aims the red laser (the enemy can see it and may step out of the line), fires, goes somewhere else and does it again. The first shot of every ultimate drops a drone where it stood; each drone takes slow, weak sniper shots on its own for the rest of the match, so the strength is in how many there are. Only five drones are allowed: once five are out, an ultimate is one slow shot instead of four, and that shot is more than 2.5 times as strong as each of the four. Its hits do not count toward the next ultimate while it is taking its shots. Five pips over the arrow show how many drones are out. It leads its target and locks at the last moment; the further away the target, the harder the shot hits (about half damage up close, one and a half times far away). It can still miss: the chance depends on how fast the target is, from almost never for a slow arrow up to about 60 to 70 percent for Blitz, the only arrow that gets away from it most of the time; Simulator, the Sniper's parry: while it is moving to its next place (the moment it is exposed) a hit on it is slapped aside, the two arrows bounce apart a little, and at once it fires three quick, weak shots straight back down the line the hit came from. Each shot stuns and knocks back for only as long as the shot lasts. Straight down the line they nearly always land, but a quick arrow that reacts can slip out of the line. It gets two parries in each ultimate. Improved: Simulator, the second wind (the boost an arrow gets when it is losing badly) is now weaker the faster the arrow is. It comes on and lasts exactly as before, but a slow arrow gets all of it and the fastest arrow in the game (Blitz) gets 60% less, because it was already too fast for the boost to be fair. Arrows in between get a share in proportion to their speed. After its four shots the Sniper pierce-dashes at the enemy to start building its next five hits; Simulator, the Sniper's look is exact rather than big: a one-pixel laser that is dotted while it tracks and solid once locked with a pulse scanning out along it, a scope that closes in and locks with one clean tick, a spark of light gathering at the muzzle, afterimages and speed lines while it moves, a hot-core tracer that runs out along the line with small shock ticks, and clean ring and spokes where it hits. Each drone is a built object (shell, arms, spinning rotors, a blinking light), lands in with a shadow and a dust ring, and has its own small laser and scope; Simulator sound: slashes (Ronin, Instinct's flurry, Thousand Cuts) now sound like clean neon cuts: a smooth rising swish of air, a bright tone that falls away like a ringing blade, and a soft body, instead of reusing the heavy impact boom. The big draw has more air and a longer ring. The Sniper has its own round low shots and soft drone ticks; Simulator on a PC window (not full screen): the picture and its controls are one block with the controls directly under the picture, and every setting is in a column on the right. The picture is sized from the room there really is (the height left under whatever is above it, less the controls, and for the wide video the width left beside the settings), so it is as big as the window allows in either shape. The controls are tighter, and the buttons never go to the side of the picture on a PC any more. Fixed: Simulator full screen on phones: the full-screen button was only shown on mouse hover, so a touch screen never showed it; it is always there now. The phone layout's own rules were also putting full screen behind other things and keeping padding round it; full screen now covers the whole screen. A wide (16:9) video on an upright phone is turned a quarter so it fills the long side, where the phone will not turn sideways for it (an iPhone); Hub for PC: after an update the app closed and did not start again. Windows ends the app the moment the installer starts, so nothing could restart it afterwards. A small hidden helper now waits until the installer has replaced Hub and starts it again if the installer did not (it does nothing if Hub is already running)

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
