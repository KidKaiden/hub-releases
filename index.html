Hub 0.16.0

New: Universal Cable is rebuilt: add the free streaming websites you use (Tubi, Pluto TV and others) and Cable shows what each one is showing, all in one place, with one search across all of them; Pick the look of Home: Netflix, Disney+, Paramount+, Prime Video, Max or Hulu style. The shows are always your websites' own; Press Play and the title opens on its website, in Cable's own window on the PC (pop-ups and ad networks blocked). If one website won't play it, Cable can look for the same title on your others. Improved: The PC app can now show pictures from websites

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
