Hub 0.20.1

New: Simulator: the logo, drawn in the arrows' own pixel style, a rainbow arrow pointing to the side with a rainbow trail on a neon grid, as a profile picture and in several sizes (apps/simulator/branding). Improved: Simulator Publish: if you are in the demo, it now says so and has a Sign in to my Hub button that takes you to the sign-in screen and back to Publish (posting needs your real account, because your YouTube and Instagram sign-ins live on your home PC). If the server cannot be reached it says what is needed and has a Try again button

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
