Hub 0.9.5

Improved: Sign-in and first-run steps slide from one to the next, and the card's height eases instead of jumping; Apps open out of the tile you tap, and Hub settles back into place when you close one; Menus grow from their button and fade away; the highlight in the side and bottom bars slides to where you tap; Changing the theme or light mode fades the whole screen across, and boxes that open in place grow smoothly; Notes and messages glide into their new places instead of jumping

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
