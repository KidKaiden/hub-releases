Hub 0.20.0

New: Simulator: 15 more arrows, each with its own way of fighting. Ascendant and Godfire transform as they land hits (Ascendant: Gold, Gold II with blue sparks, Gold III; Godfire: red then blue), with Flying Kick and Wave Beam, and Instant Transmission and Dragon Fist. Instinct dodges in phases, becomes Mastered, and every third ultimate is a legendary counter from behind: afterimages, impact frames, then a huge blue cone of a beam. Also Blitz (spin dash, burnout trail), Ronin (parry and iai cuts), Trooper (burst fire, grenades, airstrike), Visitor (abduction), Polarity, Serpent, Bomber, Swarm, Glitch, Jester, Golem and Maestro. All 32 arrows are balanced between 47% and 52% in 1v1; Simulator: four new stages: Sakura Shrine, Shattered Wasteland, Frozen Aurora and Golden Desert (dunes, mesas, cacti, an oasis, blowing sand, a tumbleweed). They are darker and calmer in the middle so the arrows stand out, with the detail at the edges; Simulator music: a song library (the built-in tracks plus your own songs, kept on this device, added one or many at a time or found in a folder) and three ways to use it: the same song for everything (it can keep running across the whole video), a random song for each piece, or a song you choose for each piece. Each clip can be given its own song in Watch, and the choice carries into downloads and posts. Improved: Simulator Watch and Publish: one clear picker for what to make. Choose the pieces (the one you are watching, each piece alone, the whole tournament, everything, or ones you tick), and for PC 16:9 and Vertical 9:16 separately choose Short, Long or both. It shows exactly how many videos that makes. The same choice is used to download and to post, a Post these button takes you straight to Publish, and the Watch page is centred and uses the width; Simulator Short videos are now just the fights, first to last, cut down, for people who only want the action. The Long cut keeps the whole story (intro, every fight, the ending). You can add the intro and the ending to the Short cut with two ticks, and they run at the Long cut's normal pace

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
