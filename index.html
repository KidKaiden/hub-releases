Hub 0.25.2

Improved: Instagram sign-in: the window now opens phone-sized and as a phone, which Instagram accepts far more readily, and if it still does not take there is a hint for what to try (Forgot password, or approve This was me in the Instagram app). Until it is connected your Instagram videos wait in the Ready to post folder.; Publish: the queue is its own full-width section of cards under the steps, with a one-line count (posted, waiting, failed), so a long queue no longer runs down one side and leaves the other empty. The captions step folds away and the other steps balance in two columns.; The Simulator is more app-like on a phone: lighter, tighter cards with the descriptions kept to two lines, a slim Randomise card and a slim full-width Run bar, less padding round every screen, and no tap flash, double-tap zoom or text selection on buttons.. Fixed: Hub for PC did not start again after an update because the helper that restarts it exited at once (it was started with the wrong flags). Fixed and tested with a stand-in program. The update that installs 0.25.2 still runs the old code, so open Hub yourself once after it; from then on it restarts by itself.; Devil and Angel were far too bright when they transformed: the flash over the screen is now darker and capped, and the wings, halo and horns are softer, so you can see them change.

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
