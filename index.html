Hub 0.24.1

New: Simulator Sandbox: New seed plays a different battle at once with the same arrows and stage, and Back and Forward step through the last five seeds (the one showing and four back). It sits with the player's own controls, so it is there in full screen and right under the picture on an upright phone; numbered buttons under the video jump straight to a seed. Keys: N for a new seed, [ to go back, ] to go forward
