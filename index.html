Hub 0.19.1

New: Simulator: the Coliseum cup, a whole tournament made of Coliseum battles. 7 to 24 arrows fight in battles of up to 6 at once, the top two of each go through, and the last few fight a final. It has every video piece (intro, each battle, finale, whole tournament), works in the Tournament, Factory and Publish screens, and the arena plans apply (for example the final in Olympus). You choose how many arrows fight in each battle (3 to 6, one number for every round or a number per round such as 4, 4, 3, evening out as arrows go) and how many arrows are introduced on one card (1 to 3, or automatic), so the opening is quick. Improved: Simulator Coliseum: now the normal fight with more arrows. Every arrow looks and acts exactly as in a 1v1 fight (same movement, abilities and ultimates), on the arena you choose made bigger, shaped for social media: a tall 9:16 stage that keeps the action clear of the apps' buttons, and a wide 16:9 version. The stadium is gone; Simulator videos: the title, rules, fighter and versus cards stay on screen about 40% longer, so the abilities and explanations can be read

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
