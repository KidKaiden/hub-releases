Hub 0.24.0

New: Simulator: 15 new arrows, 47 in all: Devil, Angel, Phoenix, Tide, Umbra, Reaper, Titan, Nova, Drake, Siren, Phantom, Luna, Dune, Hydra and Comet. Each has its own passive and ultimate and its own look: a Hellgate that drags you down, a halo ward and a judgement of light, a phoenix reborn in fire (once), a Tsunami across the whole arena, a Death Mark, a Supernova, a Meteor Strike and more; Simulator: two-tone arrows. Nearly every arrow now has a second colour on its body (one half, the tail, the tip, the wings or stripes), so they look more apart; Simulator, Judgement: when the Devil and the Angel meet in a 1v1 (the final of a tournament, or any fight between them) the stage is judged. The light drains, a sigil burns into the floor, the floor tears open and they are cast into Hell or Heaven (chosen by the fight's seed). The stage changes to a new one, both come back at full health with more health and more damage, and the realm has its own rules: Hell marks lava geysers on the floor that erupt (the Devil is at home and immune), Heaven stands columns of light that heal whoever stands in them (the Angel heals faster). The first time either is down to a quarter of its health it makes a Last Stand. The cast-in plays a little slower so it reads as an event. Improved: Simulator: the weaker original abilities are much stronger now. Lumen's flare ends with three falling spears; Singularity's black hole collapses at the end; Voltaic discharges static every third hit and ends its storm with one enormous bolt; Gale throws razors of wind and hurls you round the walls five times; Venom's poison boils over and its cloud creeps after you; Mirage's decoys burst; Magma throws fireballs and its eruption is bigger; Glacier's third chill shatters into a stun and its ice bites when it breaks; Verdant plants thorns and roots you longer; Bastion throws a shockwave when it has stored enough; Simulator: 15% less screen shake, and it settles faster when several hits land together. Reduce effects shakes even less. New sounds for every new ultimate and for Judgement
