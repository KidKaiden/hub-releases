Hub 0.26.0

Improved: Arrowhead works without your Hub: with the server unreachable (PC off, away from Wi-Fi, not synced) the Simulator still opens, runs battles and tournaments, draws the videos and downloads them to the device. Checked in the app with an unreachable server. Only posting from the Publish page needs the PC.. Fixed: Downloading a big video in the PC app (a long tournament video is hundreds of MB) could run the app out of memory, because the whole file went over as one message. It now saves in pieces straight into Downloads. Phones keep their videos a little smaller so they can save them too.

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
