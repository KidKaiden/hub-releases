Hub 0.15.0

New: Clipper: a new app that finds the big moments in finished and live streams (sound, a raised voice, a fast picture change, Twitch chat speed), cuts them into phone-shaped clips with word-by-word captions and the face on top, and waits for your tap. Approved clips go to Instagram (once you add your account) or to a Ready to post folder for TikTok; Clipper can make 8-bit videos from your ideas and remix memes. It finds the top GTA 6 streamers on Twitch and Kick for you once you add your free keys; World Link: set up from Admin with one button, finds your PC by itself, and remembers your password for each account; Universal Cable: the first screens for your shows and where they stream (sample data for now)

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
