Hub 0.24.4

New: Simulator Sandbox, New seed is now a menu with two choices: Same match (the same fighters, mode and stage, a different battle) and Mix it up (a new match from what you allow: how many fighters, which stages and which modes to leave out; your choices are kept). It is a sheet that opens the same way in full screen, on an upright phone and in the page. Improved: Simulator, Judgement: the Devil and the Angel now go through a real transformation once they are cast into Hell or Heaven. They grow by a third and take their true form: the Angel unfolds wings of light and a larger halo, the Devil spreads bat wings, grows long horns and wears a crown of embers, and a ring spreads out as they change. The banner text, the veil and the glow round both fighters are much calmer and no longer burn out the picture; Simulator, 20 more abilities made stronger (after the nine in 0.24.3): Blitz, Ronin, Trooper, Serpent, Polarity, Glitch, Golem, Tide, Umbra, Siren, Phantom, Luna, Dune, Hydra, Comet, Aeon, Frost, Fire, Iron Shell and the Regrower. More damage, faster rhythms, extra shots and stuns, bigger areas, and each hit and ultimate now throws more light, smoke and sparks; Simulator balance: the arrows were re-tuned after the changes, so none of the buffed arrows runs away with the fights; Simulator full screen on a PC: the picture now fills the whole screen. The controls are hidden until you click the picture, then go away again by themselves after a few seconds; moving the mouse does not bring them up. Fixed the app's side panel showing through the picture in full screen; Simulator on a wide PC window with the wide (16:9) video: the picture takes the left side as large as the window allows, with its controls under it, and every setting sits in its own scrolling column on the right, so the picture is never pushed off the screen; Simulator sound: the synth no longer stabs. High hiss is capped so it is a band, not an ice-pick, every tone starts softly (no clicks) and saw and square waves are filtered. Lightning, the thunder, things that fall from the sky (the warning is now a soft falling breath, not two beeps; the landing is a round thump), stun, blind, pop, clash and several ultimates were rebuilt rounder, with fewer, further-apart repeats so strikes roll instead of rattle. Fixed: Simulator Sandbox: a new seed no longer takes you out of full screen. The player is kept and the new fight loads into it; Simulator Coliseum on a wide frame: the picture is shown turned a quarter, and rising embers, falling drops, burning and healing sparks now still rise and fall the right way up on the screen; Hub > Updates, the server: Update now did nothing when GitHub had nothing newer, which is the usual case on the PC where the code is worked on (the server kept running the old code). It now notices that the code on the PC is newer than what the server started with, says so, and restarts the server on it. Pressing it once restarts the server, so the first time use update.cmd; after that the button works

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
