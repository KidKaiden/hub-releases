Hub 0.25.0

New: Blitz is now Sonic. As it fights it gathers blue electric energy and gets faster. When full it curls up and revs like a spin dash, then rolls as a ball that homes in on the enemy and bounces off it. Three bounces, then a fourth big one that hits hardest and throws both far apart. After that its speed dies and it has to build up again.; Super Blitz: after the fourth full roll, the big bounce turns Blitz golden for a while, stronger and faster, with flames standing off it.. Improved: New Blitz look: crackling arcs and Flash-style blue afterimages while it charges, a shaded spinning ball with a motion blur and trail, lightning on the big bounce, and a golden burst for Super. New sounds for the rev, launch, bounces, big bounce and Super.

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
