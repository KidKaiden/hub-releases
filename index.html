Hub 0.9.7

New: Notes > Shopping: tick-off lists. Type and press Enter to add, tick as you shop, and anything ticked waits 7 days in Recently ticked in case of a mis-tap; Repeating items: toothpaste every month, filters every 6 months, or set dates like 1 May and 1 November. Ticked, they come back by themselves on their day; Share a shopping list with your partner or a friend: you both see the same items, add and tick, and nothing is lost or doubled; Several lists (Groceries, Household...), grouped by aisle in the order you walk your shop, with suggestions from what you buy; Save a list as PDF or text, copy it into a message, or print it on the PC. Search finds shopping items too. Improved: Writing notes: checkboxes are real boxes. Tap Checklist and the line becomes one; Enter makes the next. No brackets to type; Notes has two sections, Notes and Shopping. Notebooks and Trash are inside Notes; Templates show a small picture of their layout; the Shopping list template opens Shopping. New: Packing list. Fixed: Side panels could be cut off at the bottom of short pages on the PC, and long drop-down lists could run off the screen

Installed Hubs update by themselves (Hub > Updates). New install: Windows, run the setup file; Android, open the APK (allow installs from your browser when asked).
